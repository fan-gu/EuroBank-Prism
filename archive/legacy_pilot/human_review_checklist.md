# Human Review Checklist

Complete this checklist before distributing a EuroBank Prism
report.

- [ ] Confirm the constituent universe and as-of date.
- [ ] Confirm each report is official and the URL still works.
- [ ] Confirm bank, reporting period, frequency, currency, and consolidation scope.
- [ ] Check extracted values against the source page or table.
- [ ] Investigate outliers and unit/percentage errors.
- [ ] Confirm market-price timestamps and stale-data status.
- [ ] Review missing metrics and excluded comparisons.
- [ ] Recalculate the weighted score independently for a sample bank.
- [ ] Review sensitivity results and ranking stability.
- [ ] Confirm evidence links are present for every reported fact.
- [ ] Add analyst interpretation separately from reported facts.
- [ ] Add conflicts/positions disclosure.
- [ ] Confirm the report does not present personalized buy/sell advice.
