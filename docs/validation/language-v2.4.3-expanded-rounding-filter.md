# Management-language validation — v2.4.3

Version 2.4.3 expands the standardized calculation-footnote filter after a
full-corpus audit found additional issuer-specific variants such as “all figures
subject to rounding” and “totals may not sum due to rounding differences”. It
also reapplies pollution masks after PDF line fragments are joined.

## Result

- 23/23 banks retain a current provisional language observation.
- Seven banks retain four comparable reporting periods for preliminary drift.
- One sequence remains behind the filter-shrink review gate.
- The semantic corpus audit finds zero standalone rounding-note or standard legal
  disclaimer matches across 3,179 page-cited chunks from 66 reports.

The change removes routine disclosure mechanics; it does not invert negative
phrasing or infer missing evidence. Substantive management caveats remain
eligible and every scored observation retains its source document and page.
